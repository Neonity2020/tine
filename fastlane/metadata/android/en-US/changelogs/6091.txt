Saving is much faster on large graphs: a save no longer re-reads and re-analyses
every page before writing.

Edits made outside Tine now appear for pages kept at the top level of your graph
or in your own folders, not only journals/ and pages/.

Fixes for saves that could get stuck for good: a file replaced with identical
content, a symlink anywhere in the graph, and errors wrongly shown as "changed
on disk".

Also: Ctrl+F scrolls to the exact match, update failures say what went wrong.
